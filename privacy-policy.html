Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FORMA.IT Group collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use our website, contact our team or communicate through a WhatsApp chatbot powered by our platform.

Effective date: 28 July 2026

1. Who we are

FORMA.IT Group is a technology company based in Kampala, Uganda. We provide software development, business automation, digital products and WhatsApp chatbot services.

When you communicate directly with FORMA.IT Group, FORMA.IT Group is responsible for the processing described in this policy.

When our chatbot platform is used by another business, that business may determine why your information is collected, while FORMA.IT Group processes it on that business's behalf.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages you send through WhatsApp or our website.
  • Quotation requests, project requirements and budgets.
  • Customer-support and human-handover requests.
  • Business name, email address and other details you provide.
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, delivery status and technical records needed to operate the chatbot.
  • Basic website information such as browser type, device type and pages visited, where analytics are enabled.

Please do not send passwords, payment PINs, financial credentials, medical records or other highly sensitive information through the chatbot.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • Reply to questions and customer-support requests.
  • Provide automated chatbot responses and service menus.
  • Connect a customer with a human team member.
  • Prepare quotations and follow up on project enquiries.
  • Deliver and improve our services.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Our chatbot identifies itself as an automated assistant. Customers may request human assistance at any time.

4. WhatsApp and automated processing

Our chatbot uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. Messages sent to a connected business number are delivered to our systems through secure webhook requests so the chatbot or an authorised team member can respond.

Meta and WhatsApp may separately process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Where artificial-intelligence features are enabled, message content may be processed by an approved technology provider to produce a response. Businesses using our platform are responsible for configuring the chatbot appropriately and informing their customers about its use.

5. Legal basis for processing

We process personal information where you have provided consent, where processing is necessary to respond to your request or provide a requested service, where we have a legitimate business need that does not unfairly affect your rights, or where processing is required by law.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us operate the platform, including:

  • Meta and the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers.
  • Email delivery, security and monitoring providers.
  • Artificial-intelligence providers where an AI feature is enabled.
  • The business whose WhatsApp chatbot you contacted.
  • Authorities where disclosure is lawfully required.

Service providers are only authorised to process information for agreed business purposes and are expected to protect it.

7. International data transfers

Some technology providers may process or store information outside Uganda. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to use providers with appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

8. Data retention

We retain chatbot conversations and enquiry records only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, manage customer relationships, meet legal obligations and protect our systems.

Ordinary chatbot and lead records may be retained for up to 24 months after the last interaction unless a longer period is required for an active contract, dispute, security matter or legal obligation.

9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including access controls, authenticated administration, secure network connections and restricted access to business data.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Customers should avoid sharing confidential credentials through WhatsApp or website forms.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you may request:

  • Confirmation that we process your personal information.
  • Access to personal information held about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion or destruction of information where appropriate.
  • Restriction of, or objection to, certain processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also contact Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office if you believe your information has been handled unlawfully.

11. Requesting deletion of your data

To request deletion of WhatsApp chatbot or website enquiry information, email support@formaitgroup.com with the subject Data Deletion Request.

Include your name, phone number or email address and the business chatbot you contacted. We may request reasonable verification before deleting information. Valid requests will normally be handled within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.

12. Children's privacy

Our business services are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information without appropriate permission. A parent or guardian may contact us to request removal of information submitted by a child.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
